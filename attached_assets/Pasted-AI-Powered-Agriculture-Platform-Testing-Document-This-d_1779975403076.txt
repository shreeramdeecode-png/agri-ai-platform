1. Food Security in India
India achieved a record food grain production of approximately 328 million tonnes in 2023-24. The country's food
security has improved steadily due to increased agricultural productivity, government subsidies, better irrigation
infrastructure, and technology adoption. Major food grains include rice, wheat, maize, and pulses.
2. Agriculture Commodity Prices
Commodity prices in India have shown fluctuations due to rainfall variation, export demand, fertilizer prices, and global
market conditions. Wheat and rice prices increased moderately, while pulses experienced sharp increases because of
supply shortages.
3. Weather Impact on Crop Production
Above-normal temperatures in northern India may reduce wheat yields. Timely monsoon rains are expected to
support rice and maize production in southern and eastern agricultural regions.
4. Fertilizer Recommendations
Balanced fertilizer usage is important for crop productivity. Recommended NPK ratio for wheat: Nitrogen: 120 kg/ha
Phosphorus: 60 kg/ha Potassium: 40 kg/ha Zinc Sulphate at 25 kg/ha improves grain quality and yield.
5. Government Schemes
Key government schemes for Indian farmers: - PM-KISAN - PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) - KCC
(Kisan Credit Card) - e-NAM Farmers can apply online through official portals or Common Service Centres.
6. Natural Pest Management
Farmers can control cotton crop pests naturally using: - Neem oil sprays - Pheromone traps - Bio-pesticides -
Trichoderma - Crop rotation techniques
7. Irrigation Methods
Drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation help conserve water. These methods can save approximately 40% to 60% water
compared to traditional flood irrigation.
8. Agricultural Export Outlook
India is expected to see strong export demand for: - Rice - Spices - Fruits - Marine products - Organic foods The
export outlook for the next three years remains positive.
9. Precision Agriculture
Precision agriculture technologies include: - Drones - Soil sensors - AI-based crop advisory systems - IoT monitoring
tools These technologies improve productivity and reduce operational costs.
10. Post-Harvest Management
Effective post-harvest practices include: - Sorting - Grading - Cold storage - Proper packaging - Timely transportation
These methods reduce losses in fruits and vegetables.
